# 👨‍⚕️ Skincare Consultant Guide

This guide details workflow instructions for staff users with the `SKINCARE_CONSULTANT` role.

---

## 📋 Consultant Workflow
1. **Access Consultant Workspace** (`/consultant`): View active patient directory, total assigned clients, and pending reviews.
2. **Search & Filter Patients**: Query patients by name, email, or risk rating (`Low Risk`, `Moderate Risk`, `High Priority`).
3. **Inspect Clinical Record**: Open the Patient Inspector modal to review the patient's multi-parameter diagnostic radar chart, reported allergies, and assessment score history.
4. **Schedule & Log Consultations**: Record consultation notes, barrier observations, and specialist treatment recommendations.
