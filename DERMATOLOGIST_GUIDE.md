# 🩺 Dermatologist Workspace Guide

This guide details clinical procedures for specialist users with the `DERMATOLOGIST` role.

---

## 🏥 Clinical Dermatologist Workflow
1. **Access Dermatologist Workspace** (`/dermatologist`): View high-risk patient triage queue, pending medical signoffs, and completed overrides.
2. **High-Risk Medical Triage Queue**: System automatically flags patients with Moderate Risk, High Priority, or Severe barrier evaluations.
3. **Submit Clinical Overrides**: Review AI product recommendations, adjust matching formulations, insert medical notes, and submit **APPROVED**, **MODIFIED**, or **REJECTED** decisions.
