# 👤 User Guide & Platform Walkthrough

Welcome to the **AI Skin Intelligence & Personalized Skincare Platform**!

---

## 🚀 Getting Started
1. **Register / Sign In**: Create an account or sign in using your credentials.
2. **Complete Skin Profile**: Set up your skin type (Dry, Oily, Combination, Sensitive), Fitzpatrick tone, age, water intake, and target concerns.
3. **Run AI Skin Assessment**: Take a diagnostic evaluation to receive your overall skin health score (0-100%) and personalized risk rating.
4. **Generate AI Routine**: View custom morning, evening, and weekly routine protocols tailored to your skin barrier.
5. **Check Active Ingredients**: Use the Ingredient Intelligence tool to test formulation safety and avoid active ingredient collisions.
6. **Browse AI Product Recommendations**: Explore high-suitability products filtered by budget and skin compatibility.
7. **Track Progress & Log Routines**: Record daily completed routines to build your streak, upload progress photos, and view SVG health trend lines.
8. **Export Health Reports**: Download clinical PDF, Excel (.xlsx), or CSV summary reports from the Reports & Export Hub.
