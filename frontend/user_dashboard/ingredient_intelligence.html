Educational Knowledge Base

Skincare Active Ingredient Guide

Science-backed educational breakdowns for key cosmetic compound categories.

Retinoids

Understanding Retinoids: Cell Turnover & Anti-Aging

Retinoids accelerate cellular turnover, boost collagen production, and unclog pores. Introduce gradually to avoid barrier irritation.

Niacinamide

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3): Sebum Regulation & Barrier Support

A versatile active that reduces sebum production, minimizes pore appearance, and strengthens skin barrier lipids.

Vitamin C

Vitamin C (L-Ascorbic Acid): Antioxidant Defense & Brightening

Protects skin against environmental free radicals, fades dark spots, and brightens overall complexion when used in the AM.

Hyaluronic Acid

Hyaluronic Acid: Multi-Depth Hydration

A powerful humectant that draws moisture from the atmosphere into the epidermis, plumping fine lines instantly.

Salicylic Acid

Salicylic Acid (BHA): Deep Pore Exfoliation

Oil-soluble beta hydroxy acid that penetrates deep into hair follicles to dissolve trapped sebum and clear acne breakouts.

Ceramides

Ceramides: The Skin Barrier Building Blocks

Essential lipids that make up over 50% of skin composition. Restores barrier damage and prevents transepidermal water loss.

Peptides

Peptides: Structural Signaling Chains

Amino acid chains that signal skin cells to produce collagen and elastin, improving skin firmness without irritation.

AHAs/BHAs

AHAs vs BHAs: Chemical Exfoliation Comparison

Alpha hydroxy acids (Glycolic/Lactic) exfoliate the surface for texture improvement; BHAs target interior pore congestion.
